Jingliang Li

Hi, I am a first-year Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) and a member of MARS Lab @ NTU, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Jianfei Yang. I obtained my B.Eng. degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University. Previously, I was a research intern at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and an exchange student at Efrei, Paris.

My research focuses on Embodied AI, with particular interests in 3D affordance reasoning, world models for decision-making, and dexterous manipulation.
